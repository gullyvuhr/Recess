# Recess Privacy

**Effective date:** July 29, 2026

**Publisher:** Chris Robinson

**Privacy contact:** robinson.cj@gmail.com

Recess works offline. It stores the following data locally in the app's private
database on the device:

- work schedule, work hours, cadence, and selected workdays;
- notification settings, bell sound, quiet hours, Recess duration, and exercise
  difficulty preferences;
- onboarding completion state;
- completed Recess History;
- scheduled, deferred, rain-check, active, and completed session state;
- whether each session originated from a manual start or a scheduled Bell;
- the exercise assigned to a session; and
- relevant scheduling, acknowledgement, deferral, rain-check, start,
  completion, and creation timestamps.

Recess has no servers or accounts and does not send data for analytics,
advertising, or synchronization. It does not use cloud storage controlled by
Recess.

On Android, if system backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may copy
the Recess database to the user's Google backup or another device. Recess does
not receive or access those backup copies. Native notification scheduling
caches and other transient runtime files are excluded from backup and are
rebuilt from the durable database. Notification permission and exact-alarm
access may need to be granted again after restoring to a new device.

Clearing Recess app data or uninstalling Recess removes its local database from
that device. Copies retained by Android system backup are governed by the
user's Android and Google backup settings.

## Policy updates

This policy may be updated when Recess's data practices or legal requirements
change. The effective date above will be revised when an updated policy is
published. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its store
listing when appropriate.
