# Privacy Policy for Recess

**Effective date:** July 31, 2026

**Developer:** Chris Robinson

## Introduction

Recess is a local-first wellness and productivity application that helps users schedule and take intentional breaks. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Recess.

Recess does not require an account or login. Recess has no backend services, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

## Information We Collect

Recess does not collect personal information or transmit user information to Chris Robinson or to Recess-operated servers.

To provide its features, Recess stores application data locally on your device. This may include:

- your work schedule, work hours, reminder cadence, and selected workdays;
- notification preferences, bell sound, quiet hours, Recess duration, and exercise difficulty preferences;
- onboarding completion state;
- Recess history and session status, including scheduled, deferred, rain-check, active, and completed sessions;
- whether a session began manually or from a scheduled reminder;
- assigned exercises; and
- timestamps associated with scheduling, acknowledgment, deferral, rain checks, session starts, completion, and record creation.

## How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used only to operate Recess, including to:

- maintain your schedule and preferences;
- create and deliver scheduled Recess reminders;
- manage active and deferred Recess sessions;
- display your Recess history and completion information; and
- restore the application's state after it is closed or restarted.

Recess does not use information for advertising, profiling, analytics, marketing, or tracking.

## Local Device Storage

Recess stores schedules, settings, preferences, session information, and history in the application's private local database on your device.

Recess does not provide cloud synchronization and does not upload this database to Recess-operated servers. On Android, if system backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may copy the Recess database to your Google backup or another device. This process is controlled by Android and your Google account settings. Recess does not receive or access those backup copies. Transient notification caches are excluded from Android backup.

## Notifications

Recess uses notifications solely to deliver reminders at the times determined by your schedule and preferences. On supported Android versions, the app may request notification permission, exact-alarm access, permission to restore reminders after a device restart, and vibration capability.

These permissions are used only for scheduled Recess reminders and normal application operation. Notification permission and exact-alarm access may need to be granted again after restoring the application to a new device.

## Third-Party Services

Recess does not use Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Analytics, Sentry, advertising networks, or third-party tracking services. Recess has no third-party login, hosted backend, or Recess-controlled cloud storage.

The application uses open-source software libraries to provide local application functionality. Those libraries are not configured by Recess to collect analytics, serve advertising, or track users. Android system services may process notifications and, when enabled by you, system backup or device transfer under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

Recess does not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal information to advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties. Because Recess does not receive your locally stored application data, it cannot sell or share that data.

## Data Retention

Locally stored application data remains on your device until you clear Recess application data or uninstall the application. You can also change schedules and preferences within Recess.

Copies retained through Android system backup are managed separately by Android and Google according to your device and Google account backup settings. Recess does not control the retention or deletion of those system-managed copies.

## Children's Privacy

Recess is not directed to children under 13, and Recess does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application has no accounts, backend, analytics, or tracking, Recess does not receive information that would identify a user's age.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with Recess, contact the developer using the address below.

## Security

Recess limits application data to private local storage provided by the operating system and does not transmit that data to Recess-operated servers. Access to the device, operating-system backups, and device-transfer features remains subject to the security of your device, Google account, and platform settings.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate device access controls, such as a screen lock and current operating-system security updates.

## Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Recess's data practices, platform requirements, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. Material changes will be communicated through the application or its store listing when appropriate.
